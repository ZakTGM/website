Meet Our Student Council

The Student Council at Southern Ontario Collegiate is dedicated to fostering a positive school culture, supporting student activities, and advocating for the student body. Our council is a diverse group of leaders who are committed to making a difference both within and beyond our school community.

Our Objectives

  • Promote school spirit through events and activities.
  • Provide a platform for student voices and concerns to be heard.
  • Encourage leadership, teamwork, and personal growth among students.
  • Organize community outreach and service projects.
  • Facilitate open communication between students and faculty.
  • Support extracurricular clubs and activities at the school.
